About HER SUPPORT

A trusted space built around girls.

Who we are

Guidance, respect and opportunity

HER SUPPORT is a proposed support platform designed to help girls and young women find information, explore mentorship and understand where to begin when they need guidance. The website brings key pathways together in one clear and welcoming place.

The project focuses on making information easier to navigate, presenting choices in plain language and encouraging visitors to seek appropriate help from trusted people and services.

Safety

Support should feel respectful, careful and considerate.

Empowerment

Clear information can help girls make informed choices.

Community

Build pathways towards trusted people and support.

A welcoming starting point for girls and young women seeking guidance, mentorship and information.
